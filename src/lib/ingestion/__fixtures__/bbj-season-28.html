Week-by-week Recap

10 weeks

Week 1

2026-07-07 – 2026-07-16

Dee kicked off the season with an HOH win and put up Taylor, Mallory, and Yash, but Mallory's veto win (and self-save) forced Dee to renom Ashley, who proceeded to torch her own game by blabbing the whole vote plan to half the house. That overshare turned "keep Ashley" allies into "get her out" votes overnight, and even though Yash grabbed the Block Buster to skate off the block entirely, it didn't matter because Ashley was already dead in the water. She went out 14-0 opposite Taylor, officially the first casualty of a very chatty, alliance-happy week one.

Week 2

2026-07-16 – 2026-07-23

Rick Devens went back-to-back with HoH and veto, sealing off any suspense by pulling Lyric Medeiros to safety and dropping Rome Seymour on the block as the real target the whole "Toolshed" alliance had been circling since the moment Rome and Jason walked in on their secret meeting. The Block Buster comp let Jason dodge the chop entirely, leaving Melody to sit next to Rome as the sacrificial numbers pick while Rome's fake breakup with Lyric fooled basically no one in the house. With Rome's campaigning falling flat and even his own allies like Jason and Taylor conceding he had to go, the finalized nominees left Melody as an easy stay and Rome as the expected boot, he was evicted 12-1.

Week 3

2026-07-23 – 2026-07-30

Kamu grabbed HoH and immediately went after Jason, but not before running LaLa and Mallory up as extra bait, and the whole "Tea Party" callout turned into must-see TV as Jason spiraled into full kettle-banging, spoon-breaking meltdown mode. Lyric's veto win saved herself, which let Kamu slide Jason onto the block as the real target all along, and even Mallory's Block Buster survival couldn't rescue him. The house closed ranks 12-0 to send Jason out the door.

Week 5

2026-08-06 – 2026-08-13

LaLa's first HOH swing was aimed squarely at the Haley/Chuk pairing, nominating Chuk, Barrett, and Haley and telling Chuk flat out he was collateral damage for being "tied like a knot" to a sinking Haley. Kamu grabbed the veto but sat on it, so the block never moved, and Haley clawed her way to safety by winning Block Buster at the last second. That left Barrett and Chuk as the final two, and the house sent Chuk out the door while Barrett survived another week of being everyone's favorite pawn.

Week 6

2026-08-13 – 2026-08-20

Yash grabbed HoH and immediately came for the vets, throwing up Dee, Angela and Haley before locking down the veto too, only for Devens to torch his own game plan with the Diamond Power of Veto, yanking Dee off and slapping Kamu up in her place. Angela then bailed herself out entirely by winning Block Buster, leaving Kamu and Haley to duke it out for the block after a week full of leaked powers, alliance mergers, and Devens threatening "scorched earth" if anyone crossed his new Mosh Pit. Despite days of Mallory and Melody wobbling on whether to protect their new "icons" alliance, the house ultimately turned on Kamu, sending him out the door while Yash notched a tidy HOH resume of flushing two powers and surviving the fallout unscathed.

Week 7

2026-08-20 – 2026-08-27

Dee's second HOH run kicked off with her scooping up a bribe deal with Yash and torching whatever was left of the Mosh Pit before landing on Drew, LaLa and Taylor as her actual noms. LaLa clawed her way off the block by winning veto, and Drew got Block Buster to save himself, which left Dee scrambling for a fourth body and settling on Mallory as the sacrificial renom everyone had been circling anyway. The house barely blinked at the final vote, sending Mallory out 7-2 over Taylor while the fandom was still busy dissecting Drew's cleaning comments and a missing hairbrush that apparently mattered more to some houseguests than the actual eviction.

Week 9

2026-08-27 – 2026-09-03

Drew's HoH week turned into a masterclass in indecision, as he waffled between renom options before finally putting Devens up next to LaLa and Taylor, only for Devens to dodge the axe entirely by winning Block Buster. Yash's veto win kept the wheels spinning, but the real drama was Dee and Barrett independently arriving at the exact same secret plan to blindside Devens, completely unaware they were duplicating each other's scheming until Devens' safety win made it all moot anyway. With Devens off the block, the vote came down to LaLa versus Taylor, and LaLa was sent packing 4-2 despite her late campaign push, while Taylor survived thanks to Melody and Barrett's votes in her corner.

Week 10

2026-09-03 – 2026-09-10

Barrett's HOH reign was pure chaos energy from the jump, putting up all three original Icons (Angela, Dee, and Devens) at once after Dee torched her own game by oversharing the details of Yash's bribe deal. Yash held onto his veto win and didn't use it, locking the trio on the block together and sending Devens into the Block Buster, which he won to save himself and force the vote down to Angela versus Dee. In the end the house sided with Dee, sending Angela out the door 3-2, closing the book on one of the season's longest-running alliances one member at a time.

Week 11

2026-09-10 – 2026-09-17

Yash's HOH reign ran back the all-FAP nomination trio, putting Drew, Melody, and Barrett on the block while his side-deal with Devens (and Dee's, thanks to that infamous bribe) kept both of them safely off the chopping block entirely. Drew clawed his way out with an epic marathon veto win, using it on himself, which forced Yash to renom Taylor since his hands were tied elsewhere, but Melody's Block Buster win yanked her right back off the block before the vote even happened. In the end it came down to Barrett versus Taylor, and the house sent Barrett out the door 3-1, closing the book on a rough final stretch that included a lost HOH laundry saga and some very mixed-message campaigning to both sides of the house.

Week 12

2026-09-17

Melody grabbed HoH and put up Dee, and Devens, but the whole thing shifted when Devens won veto and pulled himself off the block. That left Melody to re-nom Yash, and with the numbers tightening down to the wire, the house sent Yash out the door in a tense 2-1 vote while Dee survived to fight another week.
