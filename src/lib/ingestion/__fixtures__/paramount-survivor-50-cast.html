Survivor 50: Everything You Need To Know

Let's get it on!

By Joal Ryan | Published 8:30 AM EST, May 21, 2026

CBS

Survivor 50 streams on Paramount+. With returning players and fan-determined game design, the anniversary Survivor season delivers can’t-miss moments from the start. Jeff Probst hosts.

Read on for a spoiler-free look at Survivor 50: In the Hands of the Fans, including how to watch all episodes. Plus, get an episode guide to the season, and see photos of the full cast and the starting tribes.

Get Started With Paramount+

Where to watch Survivor?

Right now, you can watch all episodes of Survivor, from Season 1 to the just-concluded Season 50, on demand via Paramount+ with a subscription.

When the next new season, Survivor 51, premieres in Fall 2026, new episodes will air on CBS, and stream on Paramount+.

What is the Survivor 50 theme?

The Survivor 50 theme is In the Hands of the Fans, with gameplay elements having been determined by fan voting that was conducted during Survivor 48. And when we say gameplay elements, we mean fans were asked to weigh in on everything from the color of the buffs, to whether the Survivor 50 winner should be revealed at a live reunion show in Los Angeles, or on the island right after the jury’s ballots have been cast.

The starting tribes of Survivor 50 are: Cila, Kalo, and Vatu. They launch with eight players each. Below is a breakdown of which players are in which tribe as of the Survivor 50 premiere.

Who’s in the Survivor 50 cast?

The Survivor 50 cast features 24 returning players, including Survivor legends Cirie Fields, Benjamin “Coach” Wade, Ozzy Lusth, and Colby Donaldson (all castaways with at least three past appearances), and three Survivor winners, Dee Valladares (Season 45 Sole Survivor), Kyle Fraser (Season 48 Sole Survivor), and Savannah Louie (Season 49 Sole Survivor).

Survivor 50 marks the first time the show has featured returning players since 2020’s Season 40 (aka Survivor: Winners at War).
