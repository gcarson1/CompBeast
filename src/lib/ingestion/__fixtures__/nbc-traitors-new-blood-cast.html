NBC's all-new civilian version of The Traitors features a new crew of Faithfuls and Traitors, all vying for a prize of up to $250,000.

Key Takeaways

  • NBC’s The Traitors: New Blood features 22 everyday Americans competing in a civilian version of the reality competition series for a prize fund worth up to $250,000.
  • Hosted and executive produced by Alan Cumming, the game centers on Faithfuls trying to identify and eliminate hidden Traitors before the finale, while Traitors secretly “murder” contestants each week.
  • The special two-hour premiere airs Thursday, September 17 on NBC, with new episodes airing Thursdays at 8 p.m. ET and streaming the next day on Peacock.

After more than 80,000 people applied, it's time to reveal the inaugural class of Traitors and Faithfuls who will usher in NBC's all-new civilian version of The Traitors.

How to Watch

Watch The Traitors: New Blood Thursdays at 8 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock.

Stream Now on

A cast of 22 everyday Americans have been hand selected from across the country for The Traitors: New Blood. That's right: no celebrities or socialites. The special two-hour premiere hits NBC on Thursday, September 17 with two action-packed episodes. New episodes will continue to air Thursdays at 8:00 pm ET on NBC and stream the next day on Peacock.

RELATED: How to Get Cast on NBC's New Civilian Version of The Traitors (DETAILS)

Host and executive producer Alan Cumming will open up his castle in the Scottish Highlands, inviting the cast to partake in treacherous strategy, a series of exhilarating missions, and psychological adventure, all while building a prize fund worth up to $250,000. The goal? For the Faithful to sniff out the deceitful group of Traitors tasked with secretly murdering them all one-by-one each week. If the Faithfuls manage to find and banish all the Traitors by the end of the game, they get to share the prize fund. However, if even one Traitor is left by the finale, they'll take home the entire prize pot for themselves.

Let the games begin.

Kim on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

Logan on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

And on top of that, if you're a good real estate agent, your number one priority should be negotiating the best interests of the people you represent. 

I say all the time, 'I'm a representative of your best interest. I'm not a salesman. I don't sell houses. I make sure that you get what you need.' I know how to negotiate, and I'm really good at getting what I want. So, we'll see if I can relate that to this show or not."

Mark Zgoda 

Mark on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

 Photo: NBC

Hometown: Phillipsburg, NJ

Occupation: Personal Trainer

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Faithful

His approach to the game: "Phase one is all about relationships and just don't be the loudest in the room. Let people get themselves out, because odds are, someone's going to be louder or try to step up and you just be a great number two. 

Phase two is to really find the most trusted in the room, and be a great number two. Then, repeat people's ideas back to themselves so they think they're coming up with it... let people who are making the big decisions keep a target on their back. 

And then phase three, at some point you got to step up, and I want to go down guns blazing. I would like to be the person making shots. If it comes down to five or four of us, split the group up and just take home the money."

I'm definitely gonna play up some of the more palatable parts of my career and downplay some of the more ethically ambiguous parts of my career."

Morgan on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Faithful

Shane Beatty 

Shane on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

 Photo: NBC

Occupation: Ironworker

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Traitor

His approach to the game: "People look at me as a gentle giant, and I'm looking to use that as my advantage. I want to come in not seeming like I know more than anybody. I don't want to enter a castle and put on an act... but I also don't want to be the lead detective. 

I'm thinking that introducing new names to a conversation might be the smartest move. I'm looking to just play my own game, but I don't want to piggyback off of too many people. I want to separate myself from the crowd a little bit." 

Sherry Kuehl 

Sherry on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Traitor

Her approach to the game: "I've been a writer for many years. I started out as a journalist. I think one of the best things I have going for me is that I'm an excellent listener. I learned a long time ago, you learn more from listening than talking.

You need to know how to work a room, but yet nobody knows you're working the room. You have to remember what you hear, but you also have to think about what people aren't saying — which is just as important as what they are saying. Sometimes more."

Tomica Adams

But then being a pilot, working with so many people and being very in control of things; being able to hold my stress level down will be an advantage for me in this game.

I'm born and raised in Boston, and I live in the South. I went to school in Washington, lived in Arizona. Also lived in Wichita, Kansas. I think being from the South will really make people at home; really having that Southern comfort and making them relaxed in my presence."

Victor Vollbrechthausen 

Victor on The Traitors: New Blood.

 Photo: NBC

Hometown: New York, NY

Occupation: Business Executive

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Traitor

His approach to the game: "Unfortunately, my entire life it's, 'Work hard and you see the results. You put your head down in the pool, you swim, you kick harder.' But I think brute force, unfortunately, is not going to be what's going to cut it. I'm going to have to use my mind a little bit more, but I think it's more charm and charisma. I think people are gonna have to like me, they're gonna have to trust me.

From what I've seen, people really focus on the lying aspect [of the game]. That's not my forte, and that's not how I'm gonna play it. I want people to like me. I think you have to build a tribe and really have them vouch for you instead of you have to vouch for yourself. I'm gonna — hopefully — find four or five people that are gonna be my shooters, they're gonna be my ride-or-dies, and have them take me to the victory." 

Wyatt Gillespie 

Wyatt on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

Hometown: Ligonier, PA

Occupation: Designer

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Faithful

His approach to the game: "I think a little bit of the strategy is just to be myself as an individual. I think naturally, I'm a people pleaser. I make sure people are happy, I make sure they're seen, and I'm a very genuine individual. If I can make somebody feel that way, that's their disadvantage and my advantage. I think I plan to play a little bit dumb and run circles around people when they underestimate me because I will just over-deliver at the end."

Xavier Scruggs 

Xavier on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

 Photo: NBC

Hometown: Wesley Chapel, FL 

Occupation: MLB Analyst

Rather be a Traitor or Faithful? Traitor

His approach to the game: "Understand, 'When is there a time where I need to be more outspoken? When is there a time where I need to lay back?' I think that's where playing a long season in Major League Baseball helps you understand, there's going to be certain highs and certain lows, and you have to be strategic about how you're going to use your energy — exude your energy — at the right times. 

Understanding leadership as well. Knowing who's the leader, who's the top dog at a certain point. And being able to insert yourself in that same type of role. I think there's so many of those kind of parallels within baseball and within The Traitors, I think I can use to my advantage."

Who is the host of The Traitors: New Blood?

Host Alan Cumming on NBC's The Traitors: New Blood.

 Photo: NBC

It wouldn't be The Traitors without Cumming. The Emmy winner returns to Scotland for NBC's debut version for viewers.

RELATED: NBC's Fall 2026 Premiere Dates Revealed: Here's When All Your Favorite Shows Return

“We’re excited to bring along The Traitors’ highly addictive fan base as we discover the next generation of legendary gamers and schemers,” said Sharon Vuong, Executive Vice President, Unscripted Programming, NBCUniversal Entertainment. “With our resident host and gameplay master of ceremonies, Alan Cumming, on board to introduce complete strangers to new epic missions and twisted gameplay, this new version for NBC offers a unique opportunity for the cast and audience to meet each other for the first time and we know it will be incredible to watch.”  

Stephen Lambert, CEO of Studio Lambert (the producers of both the Peacock and NBC versions) added, "The Traitors format has proven itself as one of the most gripping and talked-about reality competition shows in the world. We’re thrilled to be working with NBC to open up the experience to a new group of civilian players, whose stories and strategies will make the gameplay even more unpredictable – and, we hope, even more addictive for viewers.”
