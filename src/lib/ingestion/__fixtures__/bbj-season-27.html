Season Overview

At a glance

Big Brother 27 premiered on July 8, 2025 with 17 houseguests. The season lasted 83 days, concluding on September 28, 2025. Ashley Hollis was crowned the winner, defeating Vince Panaro in the final vote. Keanu Soto was named America's Favorite Player.
